## 🔴 The correction of 29/08 was itself wrong, and this is what proves it

Version **0.6.13** carried this, and it was repeated as settled:

> *"⚠️ Correção de 29/08 — o "a Apple exige o vídeo" é alegação NOSSA, não citação
> dela. (…) O que a 5.1.1(v) pede com certeza é indicar o caminho até a exclusão;
> vídeo é reforço barato que costuma evitar uma ida e volta, não requisito provado."*

The message asks for it in as many words:

> *"reply to this message with **a screen recording captured on a physical device**
> that demonstrates: Creating a new account or signing in with the demo account /
> Navigating to the account deletion option / The complete account deletion flow from
> initiation to confirmation"*

And it says where it goes afterwards:

> *"Include the recording in the **Notes field** of the App Review Information section
> in App Store Connect for future submissions."*

**The mechanism is worth more than the fact.** 0.6.13 was right that the claim had
hardened from paraphrase into fact across four files with no source. It then
corrected the paraphrase **with another paraphrase** — a confident reading of what
5.1.1(v) "asks with certainty", written by someone who also had not read the message.
It even wrote the rule that would have caught it (*paste the literal message*) and
filed it for the **next** rejection, while the original of **this** one was one click
away in App Store Connect.

> **A wrong claim and its correction can share the same defect.** Both were written
> without opening the source, and the second one felt more rigorous because it was
> tearing something down.

## Two other things the literal text settles

- **"Review Device: iPad Air 11-inch (M3)", version reviewed 0.6.5** — Apple reviewed
  on an iPad a binary that was **already `TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY: "1"`**. So going
  iPhone-only does not keep the app off a tablet in review, and the leftover iPad
  screenshots in the listing were never the invitation. The 14/08 decision stands for
  its other reasons; this one it does not buy.
- **The 2.1(a) complaint is literally about the tap producing nothing** — *"no action
  or further response was produced after we tapped on Microphone option"*. Since
  09/09 the microphone records and transcribes, so the answer addresses the sentence
  as written, not a summary of it.
